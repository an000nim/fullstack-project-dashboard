`light-bootstrap-dashboard.css` contains selected original rules from `src/assets/css/light-bootstrap-dashboard-react.css`: wrapper, sidebar, navbar, cards and buttons. Unused demo and plugin rules are omitted.

`DashboardLayout.jsx` adapts the structure of `src/layouts/Admin.js` and `src/components/Sidebar/Sidebar.js` to the two application sections. The original demo tables are replaced by AG Grid.

The project palette, responsive layout and form styles are defined in `../styles.css`. No demo pages, chart libraries or template settings panel are included.
